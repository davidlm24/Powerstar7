DESIGN AUDIT · AUG 2026

UI design audit

9 PAGES REVIEWED · FOCUS: ICONOGRAPHY & ILLUSTRATION · HTML + CSS SOURCE, NOT JUST SCREENSHOTS

The foundation is stronger than the surface. Typography, spacing, the dark hero and the accessibility work are good and worth keeping. What undermines the site is the artwork layer on top: template-style metaphor illustrations, a generic UI icon set stretched to six times its native size, and a portfolio with nothing in it.

Your instinct about the icons and illustrations is right — and for an agency selling design, artwork is exactly where visitors judge you.

Worth keeping

Type system. One typeface (Space Grotesk), fluid scale, tight display headings.
Tokens. Consistent 10/15px radii, fluid section rhythm, disciplined palette.
Dark hero surface. The ink-to-blue/violet bloom is distinctive; keep it.
Glass components. Header pill, cards and buttons share one material logic.
Accessibility. Skip link, focus rings, reduced-motion, contrast and print styles.
Performance hygiene. Self-hosted subset fonts, no frameworks, lazy images.
01

The illustration set

HIGH

Stock metaphors. Rocket = growth, trophy = quality, bulb = ideas, pen tool = design. These are the most-used symbols in template marketplaces; they describe any agency, so they say nothing about this one.

Style clash. Flat gradient fills with near-black sticker outlines sit on top of the site's liquid-glass surfaces — two aesthetics competing on one screen.

Sparkle filler. Every artwork is dressed with the same 4-point sparkles — decoration with no content, and a look now strongly associated with AI-generated pages.

Recycled. The trophy repeats on Home and About Us; the pen scene on Home and the Web Design page.

Opportunity cost. Each illustration fills a 50% column where an agency would show its own work.

02

Iconography

HIGH

Wrong native size. The set is drawn on a 24px UI grid, then shipped at 56px in cards and ~160px on the Services page. Strokes inflate to ~10px while detail density stays 24px-coarse — a blown-up interface glyph, not display art.

No brand voice. Generic rounded-outline style; nothing connects it to your star mark or the bolt in your favicon.

Inconsistent two-tone. On some icons the blue accent is the main gesture (Branding's arc), on others a 3px dash (Hosting's dots). The accent has no rule.

Icon as hero. On the Services page, each section's second column is a single icon — half the layout is effectively empty.

04

Smaller issues

MED

🚀 emoji sits inside three primary CTAs. It renders differently on every OS and undercuts the premium glass look — "Let's get started" carries fine on its own.

MED

All four button variants hover to the same blue — the white hero pill flips identity mid-hover. Darken each variant within its own color instead.

MED

Team cards on About Us use a generic person glyph where photos belong — the same decoration-instead-of-proof pattern.

MED

Six pages ship visible ADD COPY / REVIEW REQUIRED blocks. Right as internal TODOs; launch blockers as-is.

LOW

The 58×3px blue underline beneath every heading is a website-builder tic; the uppercase eyebrow labels already do that job.

LOW

The staggered service-card columns leave a ragged bottom edge at tablet widths.

What I'd do, in order

R1

Put real work where the clipart is

Replace the hero scene with framed screenshots of 2–3 real projects — the browser panel already in your hero SVG is the right container, just with a real site inside it. Fill Our Work with real shots and outcomes. This needs materials from you.

R2

One graphic motif, taken from your own logo

The star in your mark and the bolt in your favicon are a ready-made graphic language: section markers, list bullets, a subtle background pattern. Retire the rockets, trophies and bulbs entirely.

MARK · SPARK · AS LIST BULLETS
R3

One icon policy

One set drawn for display weight, contained in a 56–64px chip, never scaled past it. On the Services page, swap the giant icon for the deliverables list or a project screenshot.

R5

Drop the CTA emoji, add team photos, and clear the ADD COPY blocks before launch — I can draft copy for any of them.
